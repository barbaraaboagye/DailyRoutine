The Evening

The Time Rule (Use AT)

"I wake up at 7:00 AM."

"I go to bed at 11:30 PM."

Phase 2: Asking about Lifestyle

Goal: Memorize these 4 questions. You will need them to interrogate your partner.

Wake Up

"What time do you wake up?"

Afternoon

"What do you do in the afternoon?"

Evening

"What do you do in the evening?"

Bedtime

"What time do you go to bed?"

How to Answer (The Formulas)

Answering Time Questions

I + [Wake up / Go to bed] + AT + [Time]

  • 1. "I wake up at 6:00 AM."
  • 2. "I go to bed at 11:30 PM."
  • 3. "I eat dinner at 7:00 PM."

Answering Habit Questions

I + [Habit] + IN THE + [Time of Day]

  • 1. "I exercise in the morning."
  • 2. "I work at the office in the afternoon."
  • 3. "I watch Netflix in the evening."

Phase 3: The "Monster S" Trap

Warning: When you talk about yourself (I), the verb is safe. When you talk about ANYONE ELSE (He/She), the monster attacks the verb.

Talking About Yourself

I wake up.

I exercise.

I watch TV.

👾

Talking About Your Partner

He wakes up.

She exercises.

He watches TV.

Do not drop the S!
