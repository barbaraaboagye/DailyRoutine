Phase 2: DO vs. DOES

Goal: To ask a question, you must check the subject. Is it one person, or many people?

DO

(Plurals / I / You)

  • Do you...?
  • Do they...?
  • Do your parents...?
  • Do David and Sarah...?

Example: "What time do David and Sarah wake up?"

DOES

(Singular / Names)

  • Does he...?
  • Does she...?
  • Does David...?
  • Does your boss...?

Example: "What time does David wake up?"

The Question Formula

What time [DO/DOES] + [Subject] + wake up?

What [DO/DOES] + [Subject] + do in the afternoon?

Phase 3: The "Monster S" Trap

Warning: When you answer the question, look at the subject. If the subject is ONE person, the monster attacks the verb.

SAFE (Plurals & I)

I wake up.

My parents exercise.

David and Sarah watch TV.

👾

DANGER (Singular Names)

David wakes up.

My boss exercises.

Sarah watches TV.

Do not drop the S!
